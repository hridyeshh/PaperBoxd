# Product

## Register

Both — product for authenticated app surfaces (bookshelf, profile, feed, discovery, diary, lists); brand for the landing page and acquisition funnel. When surfaces overlap or are unclear, default to **product**.

## Users

Readers who track what they've read AND rely on social discovery. They read 10-40 books a year, want a clean log without friction, and are motivated by what people in their network are reading. They arrive from Goodreads frustration or Letterboxd curiosity. They use the app on mobile primarily (commute, bedtime) and desktop secondarily (browsing, logging after finishing a book).

## Product Purpose

PaperBoxd is a social reading tracker: log books, build shelves, write diary entries, follow readers with good taste, and get recommendations that feel personal rather than algorithmic. Success looks like a reader opening the app immediately after finishing a book and feeling like their reading life has a real home.

## Brand Personality

Clean, social, modern. The personality of a beautifully organized personal library that's open to visitors. Not the dusty antiquarian shop (Letterboxd dark), not the spreadsheet (Goodreads dense), not the retail catalog (Amazon). Confident typography, breathing room, warmth through literary texture rather than warm color saturation.

## Anti-references

- **Goodreads**: cluttered, information-overload, dated form controls, no visual hierarchy
- **Generic SaaS**: Inter everywhere, blue primary, rounded card grids, dashboard-template feel, zero editorial character
- **Letterboxd clone**: dark film-festival aesthetic, star-field backgrounds, identical layout patterns — PaperBoxd is for books and should have its own visual identity
- **Amazon / retail**: commerce-forward, product-listing energy, no editorial personality, rating everywhere

## Design Principles

1. **Social proof is the product.** Every individual action (logging, shelving, rating) should surface community context nearby. The solo tracker and the social discoverer are the same person at different moments.
2. **Restraint is the feature.** Clean and modern means knowing what to leave out. A sparse book cover grid is more inviting than a data-dense list. Resist adding metadata that isn't pulling weight.
3. **Literary without being precious.** Personality comes from typography hierarchy and micro-texture, not from forced dark aesthetics or serif-heavy decorative excess. Playfair for display, Geist for everything functional.
4. **Earned familiarity.** Use established patterns (shelf, grid, profile, feed) but execute each with enough precision that the experience feels distinctly PaperBoxd, not a template.
5. **Discovery is the reward loop.** Every screen should offer a credible next book or next person to follow. Empty states teach; content surfaces delight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline: sufficient contrast on all text, keyboard navigation for all interactive elements, screen reader labels on interactive icons and image components. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all animations.
